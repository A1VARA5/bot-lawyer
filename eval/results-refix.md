**Answer:**

**Ruling:** With zero privileged intents your bot still gets a fixed baseline from Discord: member profiles, roles, message metadata, voice state, reactions and locale, but empty content fields and no join/leave events or presence data.

**Why:** The rule "What a bot sees with zero privileged intents" applies, effective from 2024-05-31 (inferred, drawn from a user-support article whose list still includes discriminators, removed in 2023):

> Basic user profile information of server members (e.g., usernames, avatars, banners, discriminators, and nicknames);
> Roles that members have in the server;
> Metadata about messages (e.g., day and time sent);
> The voice channel members join, as well as metadata about voice channels (e.g., muted, deafened, streaming, or have video on);
> Message reactions by members; and
> Language information selected by members in their User Settings.

Source: [Visibility of Bot Data Access (user support)](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/7933951485975-Visibility-of-Bot-Data-Access)

What is missing without intents:
- Message Content intent gates `content`, `embeds`, `attachments`, `components` and `poll` fields, which otherwise return empty. There are named exceptions where content still reaches you even without the intent.
- Guild Members intent gates real-time join/update/leave events and full member-list requests.
- Guild Presences intent gates online/offline status, activities and client platform.

From the page: the "Bot Data Access & API Objects" entry confirms this baseline is independent of privileged intents and independent of verification status, and that server members can see exactly what a bot has access to via the bot profile's Data Access tab. It also notes a coming change effective November 16, 2026 where `GET /guilds/{guild.id}/channels` will start omitting channels the bot cannot view, which is unrelated to intents but worth flagging since it changes what channel list your bot sees later this year.

The "Intent Types, Approval Criteria & Alternatives" entry (from the developer docs, page undated, snapshot 2026-09-19) adds that without Message Content you still get full content for messages your app sends, DMs to your app, mentions of your app, and replies to your app's messages when the user has "ping on reply" enabled, though not replies to slash command responses. The rules dataset separately shows three official lists of these exceptions disagree slightly (contradiction C8): the 2022 change log names only three exceptions (own messages, DMs, mentions), the gateway docs page adds a fourth (message context menu targets), and the June 2026 guide adds replies but drops the context menu item. Our ruling above gives the union of all three, per the dataset's resolution.

**Watch out:** If you later request any of these intents, remember the June 10, 2026 change: the review trigger is now 10,000 unique users across all your servers, not server count, and approved intents must be reapplied for annually.

**Counsel notes:** Zero intents is not zero data. Discord hands you plenty by default; the intents only unlock content, member events and presence.
